# Peru

This vendor entry represents the Government of Peru as a source of
national laws, regulations and regulators relevant to compliance.

## What they offer

- **National Authority for Personal Data Protection (ANPD):** the
  regulator for Peru's Personal Data Protection Law (Law No. 29733),
  part of the Ministry of Justice and Human Rights.
- **Superintendency of Banking, Insurance and AFP (SBS):** the
  supervisor of banks, insurers and pension fund administrators.
