# Luxembourg

This vendor entry represents the Government of the Grand Duchy of
Luxembourg as a source of national laws, regulations and regulators
relevant to compliance.

## What they offer

- **National Commission for Data Protection (CNPD):** the supervisory
  authority for the GDPR in Luxembourg.
- **Commission de Surveillance du Secteur Financier (CSSF):** the
  supervisor of Luxembourg's financial sector.

## Official resources

- Website: https://gouvernement.lu/en.html
- CNPD: https://cnpd.public.lu/
