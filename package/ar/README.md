# Argentina

Argentina is a country in South America. This vendor represents the national
government of Argentina and its regulators as sources of laws and regulations.

## What they offer

- **National government portal:** official information and services of the
  Argentine government (argentina.gob.ar).
- **Agency for Access to Public Information (AAIP):** the national authority
  for personal data protection and access to public information.

## Official resources

- Website: https://www.argentina.gob.ar/
- AAIP: https://www.argentina.gob.ar/aaip
