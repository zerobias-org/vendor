# United Arab Emirates

The United Arab Emirates (UAE) is a federation of seven emirates in the
Middle East. In this catalog it represents UAE federal bodies and laws that
apply to organizations handling data or providing regulated services in the
UAE.

## What they offer

- **Personal Data Protection Law:** Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021
  regarding the protection of personal data.
- **Telecommunications and Digital Government Regulatory Authority (TDRA):**
  regulator of telecommunications and digital government.
- **UAE Cyber Security Council:** national body for cybersecurity strategy
  and policy.
- **Free zone regimes:** financial free zones such as DIFC and ADGM have
  their own data protection laws.
