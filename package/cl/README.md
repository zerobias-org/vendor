# Chile

Chile is a country in South America. This vendor represents the Government of
Chile and its regulators as sources of laws and regulations.

## What they offer

- **Digital Government Division:** government digital transformation and
  services.
- **Financial Market Commission (CMF):** regulator of Chile's banking,
  securities and insurance markets.
- **Consejo para la Transparencia:** authority for access to public
  information.

## Official resources

- Digital Government: https://www.digital.gob.cl/
- CMF: https://www.cmfchile.cl/
