# Switzerland

Switzerland is a country in Central Europe. This vendor represents the Swiss
federal government and its regulators as sources of laws and regulations.

## What they offer

- **Federal administration:** official information of the Swiss federal
  government (admin.ch).
- **Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC):**
  supervisory authority for the Federal Act on Data Protection.
- **FINMA:** the Swiss Financial Market Supervisory Authority.

## Official resources

- Website: https://www.admin.ch/gov/en/start.html
- FDPIC: https://www.edoeb.admin.ch/en
- FINMA: https://www.finma.ch/en/
