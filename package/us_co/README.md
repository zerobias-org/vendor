# Colorado

The State of Colorado is a US state. In this catalog it represents Colorado
laws and agencies that regulate consumer privacy and data security.

## What they offer

- **Colorado Privacy Act:** consumer data privacy law enforced by the
  Colorado Attorney General.
- **Colorado Artificial Intelligence Act:** requirements for developers and
  deployers of high-risk AI systems.
- **Data security and breach notification:** state requirements for
  protecting and disposing of personal information and notifying breaches.
