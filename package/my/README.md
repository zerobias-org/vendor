# Malaysia

This vendor entry represents the Government of Malaysia as a source of
national laws, regulations and regulators relevant to compliance.

## What they offer

- **Personal Data Protection Department (JPDP):** the regulator for the
  Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA).
- **National Cyber Security Agency (NACSA):** the national lead agency
  for cybersecurity, including the Cyber Security Act 2024.
- **Bank Negara Malaysia:** the central bank and financial sector
  regulator.

## Official resources

- Personal Data Protection Department: https://www.pdp.gov.my/
- NACSA: https://www.nacsa.gov.my/
- Bank Negara Malaysia: https://www.bnm.gov.my/
