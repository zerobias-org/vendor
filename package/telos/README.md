# Telos Corporation

Telos Corporation is a provider of cybersecurity, identity and secure
networking solutions for government and commercial organizations.

## What they offer

- **Xacta:** cyber governance, risk and compliance automation.
- **Telos ID:** identity and biometric services.
- **Secure networks:** secure communications and network solutions.
