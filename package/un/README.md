# United Nations

The United Nations is an international organization founded in 1945 and
headquartered in New York City. Its member states work together on peace and
security, human rights, development and international law.

## What they offer

- **Sustainable Development Goals:** the 17 global goals of the 2030 Agenda
  for Sustainable Development.
- **UN Global Compact:** a corporate sustainability initiative based on ten
  principles covering human rights, labour, environment and anti-corruption.
- **UNECE vehicle regulations:** regulations such as UN R155 (vehicle
  cybersecurity) and UN R156 (software updates).
- **UNCITRAL:** model laws on international trade, including electronic
  commerce and electronic signatures.
