# Venminder

Venminder is a third-party risk management provider and part of Ncontracts.
It helps organizations assess and monitor their vendors.

## What they offer

- **Third-party risk management platform:** vendor inventory, due diligence,
  contracts and ongoing monitoring.
- **Vendor assessments:** control assessments of vendors performed by
  Venminder's experts.
- **Questionnaires and reporting:** risk questionnaires and reports for
  compliance and oversight.
