# Hewlett Packard Enterprise (HPE)

Hewlett Packard Enterprise Company (HPE) is an enterprise technology company
headquartered in Houston, Texas. It was formed in 2015 when Hewlett-Packard
split into two companies.

## What they offer

- **Compute:** HPE ProLiant servers and HPE Cray supercomputers.
- **Hybrid cloud:** HPE GreenLake cloud and as-a-service offerings.
- **Networking:** HPE Aruba Networking wired, wireless and security products.
- **Storage:** HPE Alletra and other storage and data protection products.
