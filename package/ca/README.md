# Canada

Canada is a country in North America. This vendor represents the Government of
Canada and its regulators as sources of laws and regulations.

## What they offer

- **Government portal:** official information and services of the Government
  of Canada.
- **Office of the Privacy Commissioner (OPC):** oversees federal privacy law,
  including PIPEDA.
- **Canadian Centre for Cyber Security:** the federal authority on cyber
  security guidance.

## Official resources

- Website: https://www.canada.ca/en.html
- Privacy Commissioner: https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/
- Canadian Centre for Cyber Security: https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en
