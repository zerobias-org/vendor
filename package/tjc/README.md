# The Joint Commission

The Joint Commission is an independent, not-for-profit organization in the
United States that accredits and certifies health care organizations and
programs. Its standards are published in the Comprehensive Accreditation
Manuals, such as the manual for hospitals (CAMH).

## What they offer

- **Accreditation:** programs for hospitals, ambulatory care, behavioral
  health, home care, laboratories, nursing care centers and other settings.
- **Certification:** disease-specific and other certification programs for
  health care organizations.
- **Standards and patient safety:** accreditation manuals and patient safety
  requirements that accredited organizations must meet.
- **Joint Commission International:** accreditation for health care
  organizations outside the United States.
