# Intercom

Intercom is a customer service software company. Its platform combines an AI
agent, a help desk and customer messaging.

## What they offer

- **Fin AI agent:** AI that answers customer questions across channels.
- **Help desk:** inbox, ticketing and help center for support teams.
- **Messaging:** in-app and web Messenger, email and proactive messages.
