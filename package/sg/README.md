# Singapore

The Republic of Singapore is a city-state in Southeast Asia. Its government
agencies set requirements for personal data protection, cybersecurity and
financial services.

## What they offer

- **Data protection:** the Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA), administered
  by the Personal Data Protection Commission (PDPC).
- **Cybersecurity:** the Cyber Security Agency of Singapore (CSA) administers
  the Cybersecurity Act.
- **Financial regulation:** the Monetary Authority of Singapore (MAS) issues
  requirements such as its Technology Risk Management Guidelines.
