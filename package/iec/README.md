# IEC

The International Electrotechnical Commission (IEC) is an international
standards organization based in Geneva, Switzerland. It publishes standards
for electrical, electronic and related technologies.

## What they offer

- **International standards:** including the IEC 62443 series for industrial
  automation and control system security.
- **Joint standards with ISO:** through ISO/IEC JTC 1, including ISO/IEC 27001
  and ISO/IEC 42001.
- **Conformity assessment:** systems such as IECEE, IECEx and IECQ.
