# Massachusetts

The Commonwealth of Massachusetts is a US state. In this catalog it
represents Massachusetts laws and agencies that regulate data security and
privacy.

## What they offer

- **201 CMR 17.00:** standards for the protection of personal information of
  Massachusetts residents, including a written information security program.
- **Data breach notification:** breach notification requirements under
  Massachusetts General Laws Chapter 93H.
- **Office of Consumer Affairs and Business Regulation:** oversees the data
  security regulations and receives breach notices.
