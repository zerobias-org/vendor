# Nigeria

This vendor entry represents the Federal Government of Nigeria as a
source of national laws, regulations and regulators relevant to
compliance.

## What they offer

- **Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC):** the regulator for the
  Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.
- **National Information Technology Development Agency (NITDA):** the
  agency that sets IT standards and guidelines.
- **Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN):** the regulator for banks and payment
  service providers.
