# Uruguay

The Oriental Republic of Uruguay is a country in South America. In this
catalog it represents Uruguayan government bodies and laws that apply to
organizations handling personal data or providing regulated services in
Uruguay.

## What they offer

- **Personal data protection:** Law No. 18.331 on the protection of personal
  data.
- **Unidad Reguladora y de Control de Datos Personales (URCDP):** the data
  protection authority.
- **AGESIC:** the agency for e-government and the information society,
  which also sets information security policy for the public sector.
