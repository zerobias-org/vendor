# Notion Labs

Notion Labs, Inc. is the San Francisco-based company behind Notion, a
connected workspace for notes, documents, wikis and project management.

## What they offer

- **Docs and wikis:** pages and knowledge bases for teams.
- **Projects:** databases, tasks and project tracking.
- **AI:** Notion AI for writing, search and automation.
- **Enterprise features:** admin controls, SSO and audit logs.
