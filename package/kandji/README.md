# Iru (formerly Kandji)

Iru is an IT and security platform company, formerly known as Kandji,
which rebranded as Iru in October 2025. It is known for Apple device
management and has expanded into identity and compliance.

## What they offer

- **Device management:** management and security for Apple and other
  enterprise devices (the former Kandji product line).
- **Identity:** identity and access capabilities for employees.
- **Compliance:** tooling to help organizations track and meet security
  compliance requirements.
