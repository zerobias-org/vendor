# OpenAI

OpenAI is an AI research and deployment company based in San Francisco,
California. It develops the GPT family of models and the ChatGPT
assistant.

## What they offer

- **ChatGPT:** an AI assistant for consumers, teams and enterprises.
- **API platform:** developer access to OpenAI models for text, images,
  audio and agents.
- **Research:** AI research and model development.
