# U.S. General Services Administration (GSA)

The U.S. General Services Administration (GSA) is a federal agency that
supports other agencies with acquisition, real estate and technology services.

## What they offer

- **FedRAMP:** the Federal Risk and Authorization Management Program for cloud
  services used by federal agencies.
- **Acquisition:** government-wide contracts and the Multiple Award Schedule;
  SAM.gov for federal awards.
- **Technology services:** shared services such as Login.gov.
