# Belgium

Belgium is a country in Western Europe and a member of the European Union.
This vendor represents the Belgian federal government and its regulators as
sources of laws and regulations.

## What they offer

- **Federal government portal:** official information of the Belgian federal
  government (belgium.be).
- **Data Protection Authority:** the Belgian supervisory authority for the
  GDPR.

## Official resources

- Website: https://www.belgium.be/en
- Data Protection Authority: https://www.dataprotectionauthority.be/
