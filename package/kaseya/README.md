# Kaseya

Kaseya is an IT management and security software company serving
managed service providers (MSPs) and internal IT teams. Its portfolio
includes Datto, which Kaseya acquired in 2022.

## What they offer

- **Remote monitoring and management:** endpoint management and
  automation (VSA, Datto RMM).
- **Backup and recovery:** business continuity and data protection,
  including Datto products.
- **Security:** endpoint, email and security awareness offerings.
- **IT service management:** service desk and PSA tools for MSPs.
