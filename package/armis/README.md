# Armis

Armis is a cybersecurity company that provides an asset intelligence and
exposure management platform covering IT, OT, IoT and medical devices.

## What they offer

- **Asset discovery and visibility:** agentless discovery and inventory of
  managed and unmanaged devices.
- **Exposure management:** vulnerability prioritization and remediation across
  the asset inventory.
- **OT/IoT security:** security for operational technology, IoT and medical
  devices.
