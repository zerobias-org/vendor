# Portugal

The Portuguese Republic is a European Union member state. EU regulations such
as the GDPR apply in Portugal alongside national law, supervised by
Portuguese authorities.

## What they offer

- **Data protection:** the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (CNPD) is
  the national data protection authority.
- **Cybersecurity:** the Centro Nacional de Cibersegurança (CNCS) is the
  national cybersecurity authority.
- **Financial supervision:** Banco de Portugal supervises banks and other
  financial institutions.
