# Netherlands

This vendor entry represents the Government of the Netherlands as a
source of national laws, regulations and regulators relevant to
compliance.

## What they offer

- **Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens):** the
  supervisory authority for the GDPR in the Netherlands.
- **De Nederlandsche Bank (DNB):** the central bank and prudential
  supervisor.
- **Dutch Authority for the Financial Markets (AFM):** the conduct
  supervisor for financial markets.
