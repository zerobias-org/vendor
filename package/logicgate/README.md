# LogicGate

LogicGate is a governance, risk and compliance (GRC) software company
based in Chicago, Illinois. Its Risk Cloud platform helps organizations
manage risk and compliance programs.

## What they offer

- **Risk Cloud:** a configurable GRC platform for risk and compliance
  workflows.
- **Compliance management:** controls, frameworks and audit support.
- **Third-party risk management:** vendor assessment and monitoring.
- **Enterprise and cyber risk:** risk registers, assessments and
  quantification.
