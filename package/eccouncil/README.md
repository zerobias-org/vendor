# EC-Council

The International Council of E-Commerce Consultants (EC-Council) is an
information security certification and training body.

## What they offer

- **Certifications:** credentials including Certified Ethical Hacker (CEH),
  CHFI, CND, CCISO and LPT.
- **Training:** courses and exam preparation for its certification programs.

## Official resources

- Website: https://www.eccouncil.org/
- Train and certify: https://www.eccouncil.org/train-certify/
- Certification portal: https://cert.eccouncil.org/
