# LogicManager

LogicManager is an enterprise risk management (ERM) software company.
Its platform connects risks, controls, owners and actions in one place.

## What they offer

- **Enterprise risk management:** risk assessments and risk registers.
- **Compliance management:** policies, controls and audit tracking.
- **Third-party risk management:** vendor risk assessments.
- **Incident management:** tracking and analysis of incidents.
