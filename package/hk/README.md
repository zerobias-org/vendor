# Hong Kong

Hong Kong is a Special Administrative Region of the People's Republic of
China. This vendor entry represents the Hong Kong SAR Government and its
regulators.

## What they offer

- **Data protection:** the Office of the Privacy Commissioner for Personal
  Data (PCPD) enforces the Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance.
- **Banking regulation:** the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) supervises
  banks, including cybersecurity expectations.
- **Securities regulation:** the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC)
  regulates securities and futures markets.
