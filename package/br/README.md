# Brazil

Brazil is a country in South America. This vendor represents the federal
government of Brazil and its regulators as sources of laws and regulations.

## What they offer

- **Federal government portal:** official information and services of the
  Brazilian government (gov.br).
- **National Data Protection Authority (ANPD):** authority responsible for the
  LGPD, Brazil's general data protection law.

## Official resources

- Website: https://www.gov.br/
- ANPD: https://www.gov.br/anpd/pt-br
