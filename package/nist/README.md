# NIST

The National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) is a US
federal agency within the Department of Commerce. It develops
measurement science, standards and guidelines, including widely used
cybersecurity and privacy frameworks.

## What they offer

- **Cybersecurity Framework (CSF):** a framework for managing
  cybersecurity risk.
- **Special Publications:** guidance such as SP 800-53 security and
  privacy controls.
- **National Vulnerability Database (NVD):** the US government
  repository of vulnerability data.
- **Cryptographic standards:** FIPS publications, including
  post-quantum cryptography standards.

## Official resources

- Website: https://www.nist.gov/
- Cybersecurity Framework: https://www.nist.gov/cyberframework
