# RiskProfiler

RiskProfiler is a cybersecurity company that provides external threat
exposure management using AI agents.

## What they offer

- **Attack surface management:** discovery of external assets, domains and
  exposures.
- **Third-party risk:** monitoring of vendor cyber risk.
- **Brand and identity threats:** detection of brand abuse and leaked
  credentials.
