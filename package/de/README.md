# Germany

Germany is a European Union member state. This vendor entry represents the
German federal government and its national regulators and standards bodies.

## What they offer

- **Cybersecurity:** the Federal Office for Information Security (BSI)
  publishes IT-Grundschutz and the C5 cloud criteria catalogue.
- **Data protection:** the Federal Commissioner for Data Protection and
  Freedom of Information (BfDI) supervises federal bodies under the GDPR and
  BDSG.
- **Financial supervision:** the Federal Financial Supervisory Authority
  (BaFin) regulates banks, insurers and securities trading.
