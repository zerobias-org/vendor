# Saudi Arabia

The Kingdom of Saudi Arabia is a country on the Arabian Peninsula. Its
national authorities set requirements for cybersecurity, personal data
protection and financial services.

## What they offer

- **Cybersecurity:** the National Cybersecurity Authority (NCA) issues
  controls such as the Essential Cybersecurity Controls (ECC).
- **Data protection:** the Saudi Data and AI Authority (SDAIA) oversees the
  Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL).
- **Financial regulation:** the Saudi Central Bank (SAMA) regulates banks and
  other financial institutions.
