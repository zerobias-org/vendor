# Philippines

The Republic of the Philippines is a country in Southeast Asia. Its national
government publishes laws and regulations that affect data privacy,
cybersecurity and financial services operating in the country.

## What they offer

- **Data privacy:** the Data Privacy Act of 2012, enforced by the National
  Privacy Commission (NPC).
- **Financial regulation:** Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP), the central
  bank, regulates banks and other supervised financial institutions.
- **Cybersecurity and ICT:** the Department of Information and Communications
  Technology (DICT) leads national cybersecurity policy.
