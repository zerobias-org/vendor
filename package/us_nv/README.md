# Nevada

The State of Nevada is a US state. In this catalog it represents Nevada laws
and agencies that regulate privacy and data security.

## What they offer

- **NRS Chapter 603A:** security and privacy of personal information,
  including breach notification and online privacy notices.
- **Attorney General:** enforces Nevada's consumer protection and privacy
  laws.
