# Zoom

Zoom Communications, Inc. (formerly Zoom Video Communications) is a
communications platform company headquartered in San Jose, California.

## What they offer

- **Zoom Meetings and Webinars:** video meetings, webinars and events.
- **Zoom Phone and Contact Center:** cloud phone system and contact center.
- **Zoom Workplace:** team chat, docs, whiteboards and AI Companion.
- **Developer platform:** APIs and SDKs for integrating Zoom.
