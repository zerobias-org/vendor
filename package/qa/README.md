# Qatar

The State of Qatar is a country on the Arabian Peninsula. Its government sets
national requirements for cybersecurity, personal data protection and
financial services.

## What they offer

- **Cybersecurity:** the National Cyber Security Agency (NCSA) issues national
  cybersecurity policies and standards.
- **Data protection:** Law No. 13 of 2016 on the protection of personal data
  privacy.
- **Financial regulation:** Qatar Central Bank (QCB) regulates banks and other
  financial institutions.
