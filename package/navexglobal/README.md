# NAVEX

NAVEX (formerly NAVEX Global) is a risk and compliance management
software and services company. It is known for ethics hotlines and
policy management.

## What they offer

- **Whistleblowing and incident management:** EthicsPoint hotline and
  case management.
- **Policy management:** PolicyTech for policy and procedure lifecycle.
- **Third-party risk management:** due diligence and monitoring of
  third parties.
- **Compliance training:** ethics and compliance training content.
