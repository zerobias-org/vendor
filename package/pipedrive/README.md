# Pipedrive

Pipedrive is a customer relationship management (CRM) company that makes a
sales pipeline tool for small and mid-sized businesses.

## What they offer

- **Sales CRM:** visual pipeline management for deals, contacts and activities.
- **Automation and reporting:** workflow automation, forecasting and sales
  reporting.
- **Integrations and API:** a marketplace of integrations and a public API.
