# Austria

Austria is a country in Central Europe and a member of the European Union.
This vendor represents the Austrian federal government and its regulators as
sources of laws and regulations.

## What they offer

- **Federal government portal:** official information and services for
  citizens (oesterreich.gv.at).
- **EU regulations:** as an EU member state, Austria applies EU regulations
  such as the GDPR.

## Official resources

- Website: https://www.oesterreich.gv.at/en
