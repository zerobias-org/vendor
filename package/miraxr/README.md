# MiraXR

MiraXR builds MIRA, an immersive intelligence platform based in Austin,
Texas. MIRA combines digital twins, AI and spatial computing to help
enterprise teams train, plan and operate.

## What they offer

- **Training:** rehearsal of scenarios such as outages and
  cyberattacks.
- **Software lineage and audit:** visual tracking of SBOMs and
  standards.
- **Security visualization:** spatial views of attack surfaces and
  threats.
- **Operations:** command center views of real-time data.
