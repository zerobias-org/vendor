# TISAX

TISAX (Trusted Information Security Assessment Exchange) is the information
security assessment and exchange mechanism of the automotive industry. It is
operated by the ENX Association on behalf of the German Association of the
Automotive Industry (VDA).

## What they offer

- **TISAX assessments:** information security, prototype protection and data
  protection assessments of suppliers and service providers, performed by
  accredited audit providers.
- **VDA ISA catalog:** the VDA Information Security Assessment questionnaire
  used as the basis for TISAX assessments.
- **Result exchange:** a shared platform where assessed companies publish
  their TISAX labels to their business partners.
