# Wrike

Wrike is a collaborative work management platform. Teams use it to plan,
track and deliver projects and cross-functional work.

## What they offer

- **Project and work management:** tasks, projects, Gantt charts, boards and
  dashboards.
- **Workflows and automation:** custom workflows, request forms and
  automation rules.
- **Resource management:** workload planning and time tracking.
- **Integrations and API:** connectors to business tools and a REST API.
