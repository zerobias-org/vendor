# AIUC

AIUC (Artificial Intelligence Underwriting Company) maintains AIUC-1, a
security, safety and reliability standard for AI agents, developed with a
consortium of industry contributors.

## What they offer

- **AIUC-1 standard:** requirements for AI agent security, safety,
  reliability, accountability and data privacy.
- **AIUC-1 certification:** independent audits that certify AI agents against
  AIUC-1.
