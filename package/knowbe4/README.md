# KnowBe4

KnowBe4, Inc. is a security awareness training company headquartered in
Clearwater, Florida. Its platform addresses human risk through training,
simulated phishing and email security.

## What they offer

- **Security awareness training:** a library of training content for
  employees.
- **Simulated phishing:** phishing tests to measure and reduce user risk.
- **Email security:** products that detect and respond to malicious
  email.
- **Compliance training:** content for regulatory and policy training.
