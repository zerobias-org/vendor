# Zendesk

Zendesk, Inc. is a customer service software company headquartered in San
Francisco, California.

## What they offer

- **Ticketing and help desk:** omnichannel ticketing for email, chat,
  messaging, voice and social channels.
- **Help center:** knowledge base and self-service portals.
- **AI agents:** AI-powered automation for customer conversations.
- **Workforce engagement and QA:** quality assurance and workforce
  management for support teams.
