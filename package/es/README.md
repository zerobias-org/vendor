# Spain

Spain is a European Union member state. This vendor entry represents the
Spanish government and its national regulators.

## What they offer

- **Data protection:** the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD) supervises
  GDPR compliance.
- **Cybersecurity:** the National Security Framework (Esquema Nacional de
  Seguridad, ENS) applies to public sector systems and their suppliers; INCIBE
  is the national cybersecurity institute.
