# Fusion Risk Management

Fusion Risk Management is a software company that provides operational
resilience, business continuity and risk management solutions.

## What they offer

- **Operational resilience:** mapping of business services and dependencies to
  understand impact of disruptions.
- **Business continuity and crisis management:** continuity planning, incident
  response and crisis management.
- **Risk management:** third-party and enterprise risk management.
