# Finland

Finland is a Nordic country and European Union member state. This vendor entry
represents the Finnish government and its national regulators.

## What they offer

- **Cybersecurity:** the National Cyber Security Centre Finland (NCSC-FI),
  part of Traficom, provides guidance and incident response.
- **Data protection:** the Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman supervises
  GDPR compliance.
- **Financial supervision:** the Financial Supervisory Authority (FIN-FSA)
  regulates the financial sector.
