# Australia

Australia is a country in Oceania. This vendor represents the Australian
federal government and its regulators as sources of laws and regulations.

## What they offer

- **Federal government portal:** official information and services of the
  Australian government.
- **myGov:** online access to Australian government services.

## Official resources

- Website: https://www.australia.gov.au/
- myGov: https://my.gov.au/
