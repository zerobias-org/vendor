# Risk.net

Risk.net is a publication covering financial risk management, derivatives and
regulation. It is part of Infopro Digital.

## What they offer

- **News and analysis:** coverage of risk management, derivatives and
  financial regulation.
- **Rankings and awards:** industry benchmarks and awards for risk
  practitioners.
