# European Union

The European Union (EU) is a political and economic union of European member
states. EU law sets common requirements for privacy, cybersecurity and digital
services across its members.

## What they offer

- **Regulations:** GDPR, NIS2, DORA, the Cyber Resilience Act and the AI Act.
- **Cybersecurity:** the EU Agency for Cybersecurity (ENISA) supports policy
  and certification schemes.
- **Data protection:** the European Data Protection Board (EDPB) ensures
  consistent application of the GDPR.
