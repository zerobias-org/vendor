# NAIC

The National Association of Insurance Commissioners (NAIC) is the US
standard-setting organization created and governed by the chief
insurance regulators of the states, the District of Columbia and US
territories.

## What they offer

- **Model laws and regulations:** templates that states can adopt,
  including the Insurance Data Security Model Law.
- **Regulatory support:** financial analysis, data and tools for state
  insurance regulators.
- **Consumer resources:** insurance information for the public.
