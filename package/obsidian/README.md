# Obsidian

Obsidian is a note-taking and knowledge management app that stores notes
as local Markdown files and links them together.

## What they offer

- **Note-taking app:** desktop and mobile apps for linked Markdown notes.
- **Plugins and themes:** a large ecosystem of community plugins.
- **Sync and Publish:** paid services for syncing notes across devices
  and publishing notes as websites.
