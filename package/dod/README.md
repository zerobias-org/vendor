# U.S. Department of Defense (DoD)

The United States Department of Defense (DoD) is the U.S. federal executive
department responsible for the armed forces. It sets cybersecurity
requirements for its systems and for the defense industrial base.

## What they offer

- **CMMC:** the Cybersecurity Maturity Model Certification program for defense
  contractors.
- **DFARS:** Defense Federal Acquisition Regulation Supplement clauses,
  including requirements for safeguarding controlled unclassified information.
- **Security guidance:** STIGs, SRGs and the Cloud Computing SRG, published
  through the DoD Cyber Exchange.
