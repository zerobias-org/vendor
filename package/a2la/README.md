# A2LA

The American Association for Laboratory Accreditation (A2LA) is a US-based
nonprofit accreditation body. It accredits testing and calibration
laboratories, inspection bodies, certification bodies and other conformity
assessment organizations.

## What they offer

- **Laboratory accreditation:** accreditation of testing and calibration
  laboratories to ISO/IEC 17025.
- **Inspection and certification bodies:** accreditation of inspection bodies
  (ISO/IEC 17020) and product, management system and personnel certification
  bodies.
- **FedRAMP 3PAO accreditation:** accreditation of Third Party Assessment
  Organizations for the US FedRAMP program.
