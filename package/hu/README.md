# Hungary

Hungary is a Central European country and European Union member state. This
vendor entry represents the Hungarian government and its national regulators.

## What they offer

- **Data protection:** the National Authority for Data Protection and Freedom
  of Information (NAIH) supervises GDPR compliance.
- **Cybersecurity:** the National Cybersecurity Institute (NKI) handles
  national incident response.
- **Financial supervision:** the Central Bank of Hungary (MNB) supervises the
  financial sector.
