# Denmark

Denmark is a Nordic country and European Union member state. This vendor entry
represents the Danish government and its national regulators.

## What they offer

- **Data protection:** the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet)
  supervises GDPR compliance.
- **Digital government:** the Agency for Digital Government
  (Digitaliseringsstyrelsen) runs national digital government infrastructure.

- Website: https://denmark.dk/
