# Software Engineering Institute (SEI)

The Software Engineering Institute is a federally funded research and
development center operated by Carnegie Mellon University in Pittsburgh,
Pennsylvania. It conducts research in software engineering, cybersecurity and
artificial intelligence.

## What they offer

- **CERT Division:** cybersecurity research, vulnerability coordination and
  incident response practice.
- **Software engineering research:** software architecture, acquisition and
  assurance.
- **AI engineering:** research on building reliable AI systems.
