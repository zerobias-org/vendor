# Kenya

This vendor entry represents the Government of Kenya as a source of
national laws, regulations and regulators relevant to compliance.

## What they offer

- **Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC):** the regulator
  for the Data Protection Act, 2019.
- **Communications Authority of Kenya:** the regulator for the ICT
  sector, including cybersecurity oversight through its national CERT.

## Official resources

- Website: https://www.president.go.ke/
- Office of the Data Protection Commissioner: https://www.odpc.go.ke/
- Communications Authority of Kenya: https://www.ca.go.ke/
