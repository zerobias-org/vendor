# TOPdesk

TOPdesk is a Dutch software company that provides service management
software. Its platform is used by IT, facilities, HR and other shared
service departments to handle requests, incidents and assets.

## What they offer

- **IT service management:** incident, change, problem, asset and knowledge
  management aligned with ITIL practices.
- **Enterprise service management:** service desk tooling for facilities,
  HR and other departments on the same platform.
- **Self-service portal:** a portal where end users submit and follow up on
  requests.
- **APIs:** REST APIs for integrating TOPdesk with other systems.
