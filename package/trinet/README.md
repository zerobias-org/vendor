# TriNet

TriNet is a professional employer organization (PEO) based in Dublin,
California. It provides full-service HR to small and medium-sized businesses
in the United States.

## What they offer

- **PEO services:** co-employment with access to large-group benefits,
  payroll, HR expertise and compliance support.
- **Payroll and HR software:** payroll processing, tax filing, time and
  attendance and employee self-service.
- **Benefits:** health, retirement and other employee benefit plans.
- **Risk and compliance:** support with employment law compliance,
  workers' compensation and risk mitigation.
