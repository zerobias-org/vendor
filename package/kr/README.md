# South Korea

This vendor entry represents the Government of the Republic of Korea
as a source of national laws, regulations and regulators relevant to
compliance.

## What they offer

- **Personal Information Protection Commission (PIPC):** the regulator
  for the Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA).
- **Korea Internet & Security Agency (KISA):** the agency supporting
  internet security and the ISMS-P certification scheme.
- **National law database:** official texts of Korean laws.

## Official resources

- Korea policy portal: https://www.korea.kr/
- National Law Information Center: https://www.law.go.kr/
