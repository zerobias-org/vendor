# SuperOps

SuperOps provides an endpoint management platform for IT teams and managed
service providers (MSPs).

## What they offer

- **RMM:** remote monitoring and management with patching and automation.
- **PSA:** professional services automation, ticketing and billing for MSPs.
- **IT management:** asset and endpoint management for internal IT teams.
