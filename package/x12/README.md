# X12

X12 is a nonprofit standards development organization accredited by the
American National Standards Institute (ANSI). It develops and maintains the
X12 standards for electronic data interchange (EDI).

## What they offer

- **X12 EDI standards:** transaction sets for business documents such as
  purchase orders, invoices and shipping notices.
- **Health care transactions:** the X12 transactions adopted under HIPAA,
  such as claims (837), remittance (835) and eligibility (270/271).
- **Code lists:** external code lists maintained for use with X12
  transactions.
