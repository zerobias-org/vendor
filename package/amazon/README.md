# Amazon

Amazon.com, Inc. is a technology and e-commerce company headquartered in
Seattle, Washington. Its businesses include online retail, cloud computing
through Amazon Web Services (AWS), devices and digital media.

## What they offer

- **Amazon Web Services (AWS):** cloud computing, storage, database, security
  and AI services.
- **Retail and marketplace:** online retail and third-party seller
  marketplace.
- **Devices and media:** Kindle, Echo devices, Prime Video and other digital
  services.
