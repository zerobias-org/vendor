# HaloPSA

HaloPSA is a professional services automation (PSA) product from Halo Service
Solutions, built for managed service providers. The company also makes
HaloITSM and HaloCRM.

## What they offer

- **HaloPSA:** ticketing, contracts, billing, projects and asset management
  for service providers.
- **HaloITSM:** IT service management for internal IT teams.
- **HaloCRM:** customer relationship management.
