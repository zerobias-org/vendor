# CAICO

CAICO (CMMC Assessor and Instructor Certification Organization) credentials
individuals in the US Department of Defense CMMC ecosystem. ISACA serves as
CAICO under authorization from The Cyber AB.

## What they offer

- **Assessor credentials:** Certified CMMC Professional (CCP), Certified CMMC
  Assessor (CCA) and Lead CMMC Certified Assessor.
- **Instructor credentials:** CMMC Certified Instructor (CCI).
- **Training and exams:** CMMC training and certification exams.
