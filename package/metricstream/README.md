# MetricStream

MetricStream is a governance, risk and compliance (GRC) software
company. Its platform helps organizations automate enterprise-wide GRC
programs.

## What they offer

- **Enterprise and operational risk management:** risk identification,
  assessment and monitoring.
- **Compliance management:** regulatory and policy compliance.
- **Internal audit:** audit planning and execution.
- **IT and cyber risk:** IT risk, third-party risk and cyber
  compliance.
