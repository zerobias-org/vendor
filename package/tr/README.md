# Turkey

The Republic of Türkiye (Turkey) is a country spanning southeastern Europe
and western Asia. In this catalog it represents Turkish government bodies and
national laws that apply to organizations handling data or operating
regulated services in Türkiye.

## What they offer

- **Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK):** supervises the Personal Data
  Protection Law No. 6698.
- **Information and Communication Technologies Authority (BTK):** regulates
  electronic communications and information technologies.
- **e-Government Gateway (e-Devlet):** central portal for government
  services.
