# Norton

Norton is a consumer cybersecurity brand of Gen Digital Inc., the
company formerly known as NortonLifeLock. It offers antivirus, privacy
and identity protection products.

## What they offer

- **Device security:** Norton antivirus and Norton 360 security suites.
- **Privacy:** Norton VPN and privacy monitoring.
- **Identity protection:** LifeLock identity theft protection.
- **Scam protection:** tools to detect online scams.
