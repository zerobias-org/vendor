# Surfshark

Surfshark is a cybersecurity company best known for its VPN service. It also
offers identity protection and antivirus products.

## What they offer

- **VPN:** encrypted connections and IP masking.
- **Antivirus:** device protection.
- **Identity protection:** breach alerts and identity theft protection.
