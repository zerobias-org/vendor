# Bahamas

The Commonwealth of The Bahamas is an island country in the Atlantic Ocean.
This vendor represents the Government of The Bahamas and its regulators as
sources of laws and regulations.

## What they offer

- **Government portal:** official information and services of the Government
  of The Bahamas.
- **Central Bank of The Bahamas:** central bank and regulator of banks and
  trust companies.

## Official resources

- Website: https://www.bahamas.gov.bs/
- Central Bank of The Bahamas: https://www.centralbankbahamas.com/
