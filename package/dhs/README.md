# U.S. Department of Homeland Security (DHS)

The United States Department of Homeland Security (DHS) is a U.S. federal
executive department responsible for public security, including cybersecurity,
border security and emergency management.

## What they offer

- **Cybersecurity:** the Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency
  (CISA) publishes guidance, advisories and the Known Exploited
  Vulnerabilities catalog.
- **Components:** agencies including CISA, TSA, FEMA, CBP, USCIS and the U.S.
  Coast Guard.
