# KarmaCheck

KarmaCheck is a background check and screening company. Its platform
helps employers, especially staffing firms, run pre-employment checks
and move candidates through hiring faster.

## What they offer

- **Background checks:** criminal history and other pre-employment
  screening.
- **Verifications:** employment, education and related candidate
  verifications.
- **Candidate experience:** a mobile-friendly flow for candidates to
  complete and track their checks.
