# Midmark

Midmark Corporation is a manufacturer and supplier of healthcare
products, equipment and diagnostic software for the medical, dental and
veterinary markets.

## What they offer

- **Medical equipment:** exam tables, seating, lighting and casework.
- **Dental and veterinary equipment:** equipment and technology for
  dental and animal health practices.
- **Diagnostic devices and software:** vital signs, ECG and other
  diagnostic devices with connected software.
- **Workflow solutions:** real-time locating and clinical workflow
  technology.
