# The White House

The White House is the official residence and workplace of the President of
the United States in Washington, D.C. It is home to the Executive Office of
the President, which issues executive orders and federal policy.

## What they offer

- **Executive orders and presidential actions:** directives that set federal
  policy, including on cybersecurity and AI.
- **Office of Management and Budget (OMB):** issues memoranda and guidance
  that federal agencies must follow.
- **Office of the National Cyber Director (ONCD):** coordinates national
  cybersecurity policy and strategy.

## Official resources

- Website: https://www.whitehouse.gov/
- OMB: https://www.whitehouse.gov/omb/
