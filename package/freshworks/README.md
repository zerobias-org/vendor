# Freshworks

Freshworks Inc. is a software-as-a-service company that makes customer
service, IT service and sales software for businesses.

## What they offer

- **Freshdesk:** customer service and helpdesk software.
- **Freshservice:** IT service management (ITSM) and enterprise service
  management.
- **Developer platform:** APIs and an app framework for extending Freshworks
  products.
