# Proofpoint

Proofpoint, Inc. is a cybersecurity and compliance company focused on
protecting people and data, particularly through email and collaboration
security.

## What they offer

- **Email and threat protection:** defense against phishing, malware and
  business email compromise.
- **Data security:** data loss prevention and insider risk management.
- **Security awareness:** training and phishing simulation.
- **Compliance and archiving:** archiving, e-discovery and supervision.
