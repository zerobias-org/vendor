# Miro

Miro is a visual collaboration company. Its online whiteboard platform
lets teams brainstorm, plan and design together.

## What they offer

- **Online whiteboard:** an infinite canvas for diagrams, sticky notes
  and planning.
- **Templates:** ready-made boards for workshops, roadmaps and
  retrospectives.
- **AI features:** AI assistance on the canvas.
- **Integrations:** connections to tools such as Jira, Slack and
  Microsoft Teams.
