# Paycom

Paycom Software, Inc. is a payroll and human capital management software
company headquartered in Oklahoma City, Oklahoma.

## What they offer

- **Payroll:** single-database payroll, including employee-driven payroll
  (Beti).
- **HR management:** onboarding, documents and compliance tracking.
- **Talent management:** recruiting, performance and learning.
- **Time and labor management:** time tracking and scheduling.
