# Japan

This vendor entry represents the Government of Japan as a source of
national laws, regulations and regulators relevant to compliance.

## What they offer

- **Personal Information Protection Commission (PPC):** the independent
  regulator that oversees the Act on the Protection of Personal
  Information (APPI).
- **Digital Agency:** the government agency leading Japan's digital
  government policy.

## Official resources

- Website: https://www.japan.go.jp/
- Personal Information Protection Commission: https://www.ppc.go.jp/en/
- Digital Agency: https://www.digital.go.jp/en
