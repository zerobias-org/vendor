# Norway

This vendor entry represents the Government of Norway as a source of
national laws, regulations and regulators relevant to compliance.

## What they offer

- **Norwegian Data Protection Authority (Datatilsynet):** the
  supervisory authority for the GDPR in Norway, which applies it through
  the EEA Agreement.
- **Financial Supervisory Authority of Norway (Finanstilsynet):** the
  supervisor of financial institutions and markets.

## Official resources

- Norway (official portal): https://www.norway.no/
- Datatilsynet: https://www.datatilsynet.no/
- Finanstilsynet: https://www.finanstilsynet.no/
