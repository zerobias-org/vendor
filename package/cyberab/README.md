# The Cyber AB

The Cyber AB is the official accreditation body for the US Department of
Defense Cybersecurity Maturity Model Certification (CMMC) program.

## What they offer

- **C3PAO authorization:** authorization and accreditation of CMMC Third-Party
  Assessment Organizations.
- **Ecosystem oversight:** oversight of Registered Practitioner Organizations
  and the CMMC Marketplace.
- **Professional credentialing:** oversight of individual CMMC credentials
  through CAICO.
