# monday.com

monday.com is a work management software company headquartered in Tel
Aviv, Israel. Its platform helps teams manage projects, workflows and
business processes.

## What they offer

- **Work management:** boards, automations and dashboards for projects
  and workflows.
- **CRM:** monday CRM for sales pipelines.
- **Software development:** monday dev for product and engineering
  teams.
- **Service management:** monday service for IT and support teams.
