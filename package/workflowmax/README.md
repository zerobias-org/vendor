# WorkflowMax

WorkflowMax is a cloud job and project management application for
professional services and trade businesses.

## What they offer

- **Job management:** jobs, tasks and project tracking from quote to
  invoice.
- **Time tracking and timesheets:** recording billable time against jobs.
- **Quoting and invoicing:** estimates, quotes and customised invoices.
- **Accounting integrations:** integrations with Xero and QuickBooks.
