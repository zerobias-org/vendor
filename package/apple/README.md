# Apple

Apple Inc. is a technology company headquartered in Cupertino, California. It
designs consumer electronics, operating systems and software services.

## What they offer

- **Devices:** iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Watch and other hardware.
- **Platforms:** iOS, iPadOS, macOS, watchOS and related developer tools and
  frameworks.
- **Enterprise deployment:** device management and deployment features for
  business and education.
