# Poland

The Republic of Poland is a European Union member state. EU regulations such
as the GDPR apply in Poland alongside national law, supervised by Polish
authorities.

## What they offer

- **Data protection:** the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO) supervises
  GDPR compliance in Poland.
- **Financial supervision:** the Polish Financial Supervision Authority (KNF)
  supervises banks, insurers and capital markets.
- **Digital affairs:** the Ministry of Digital Affairs leads national
  cybersecurity and digitalization policy.
