# Kantata

Kantata is a professional services automation (PSA) software company,
formed in 2022 from the merger of Mavenlink and Kimble Applications.
Its platform helps services organizations plan, staff and deliver
client projects.

## What they offer

- **Professional services automation:** project delivery, time
  tracking and financial management for services firms.
- **Resource management:** scoping, resourcing and forecasting across
  teams and projects.
- **Business insights:** reporting on utilization, margins and project
  health.
