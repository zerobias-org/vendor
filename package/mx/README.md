# Mexico

This vendor entry represents the Government of Mexico as a source of
national laws, regulations and regulators relevant to compliance.

## What they offer

- **National Banking and Securities Commission (CNBV):** the supervisor
  of Mexico's banking and securities sectors.
- **Bank of Mexico (Banxico):** the central bank, which regulates
  payment systems.

- Website: https://www.gob.mx/
- CNBV: https://www.gob.mx/cnbv
