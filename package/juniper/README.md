# Juniper Networks

Juniper Networks is a networking company known for its routers,
switches, firewalls and AI-driven network management. It has been part
of Hewlett Packard Enterprise (HPE) since HPE completed its acquisition
in 2025.

## What they offer

- **Routing and switching:** Junos OS-based routers and switches for
  enterprise, data center and service provider networks.
- **AI-driven networking:** Juniper Mist for wired, wireless and WAN
  operations.
- **Network security:** SRX Series firewalls and related security
  services.
