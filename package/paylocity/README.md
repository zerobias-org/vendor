# Paylocity

Paylocity is a payroll and human capital management software company
headquartered in Schaumburg, Illinois.

## What they offer

- **Payroll:** payroll processing and tax services.
- **HR and benefits:** HR management and benefits administration.
- **Talent management:** recruiting, onboarding and performance.
- **Employee experience:** communication and engagement tools.
