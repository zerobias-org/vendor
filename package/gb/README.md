# United Kingdom

The United Kingdom is a European country made up of England, Scotland, Wales
and Northern Ireland. This vendor entry represents the UK government and its
national regulators.

## What they offer

- **Cybersecurity:** the National Cyber Security Centre (NCSC) provides
  guidance and runs the Cyber Essentials certification scheme.
- **Data protection:** the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) enforces UK
  GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.
- **Financial regulation:** the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) regulates
  financial services firms.
