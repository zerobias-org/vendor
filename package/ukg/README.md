# UKG

UKG (Ultimate Kronos Group) is a human capital management and workforce
management software company. It was formed in 2020 by the merger of
Ultimate Software and Kronos Incorporated.

## What they offer

- **HR and payroll:** human resources, payroll and talent management through
  UKG Pro and UKG Ready.
- **Workforce management:** time and attendance, scheduling and labor
  management.
- **Employee experience:** tools for employee engagement and communication.
