# India

India is a South Asian country. This vendor entry represents the Government of
India and its national regulators.

## What they offer

- **Data protection:** the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023,
  administered under the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology
  (MeitY).
- **Cybersecurity:** the Indian Computer Emergency Response Team (CERT-In)
  issues directions and handles incident reporting.
- **Financial regulation:** the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) sets IT and
  cybersecurity requirements for regulated entities.

- Website: https://www.india.gov.in/
- MeitY: https://www.meity.gov.in/
