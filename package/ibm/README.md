# IBM

International Business Machines Corporation (IBM) is a technology company
headquartered in Armonk, New York. It provides hybrid cloud, AI,
infrastructure and consulting services.

## What they offer

- **Hybrid cloud:** IBM Cloud and Red Hat software, including OpenShift.
- **AI:** the watsonx AI and data platform.
- **Infrastructure:** IBM Z mainframes, Power servers and storage.
- **Consulting:** IBM Consulting for business and technology services.
