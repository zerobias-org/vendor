# Greece

Greece is a European Union member state. This vendor entry represents the
Greek government and its national regulators.

## What they offer

- **Data protection:** the Hellenic Data Protection Authority supervises GDPR
  compliance.
- **Cybersecurity:** the National Cybersecurity Authority sets the national
  cybersecurity strategy.

## Official resources

- Hellenic Data Protection Authority: https://www.dpa.gr/
- National Cybersecurity Authority: https://www.cyber.gov.gr/
