# Stryker

Stryker Corporation is a medical technology company headquartered in
Kalamazoo, Michigan. Its products span MedSurg, Neurotechnology and
Orthopaedics.

## What they offer

- **MedSurg:** surgical equipment, emergency care and hospital products.
- **Neurotechnology:** devices for neurovascular and neurosurgical care.
- **Orthopaedics:** joint replacement, trauma and spine products.
