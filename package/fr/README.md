# France

France is a European Union member state. This vendor entry represents the
French government and its national regulators.

## What they offer

- **Cybersecurity:** the National Cybersecurity Agency of France (ANSSI) sets
  security guidance and qualifications such as SecNumCloud.
- **Data protection:** the Commission nationale de l'informatique et des
  libertés (CNIL) supervises GDPR compliance.

## Official resources

- Public services portal: https://www.service-public.fr/
- ANSSI: https://cyber.gouv.fr/
- CNIL: https://www.cnil.fr/
