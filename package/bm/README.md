# Bermuda

Bermuda is a British Overseas Territory in the North Atlantic. This vendor
represents the Government of Bermuda and its regulators as sources of laws and
regulations.

## What they offer

- **Government portal:** official information and services of the Government
  of Bermuda.
- **Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA):** regulator of Bermuda's financial
  services sector.
- **Privacy Commissioner:** supervisory authority for the Personal Information
  Protection Act (PIPA).

## Official resources

- Website: https://www.gov.bm/
- Bermuda Monetary Authority: https://www.bma.bm/
