# Serbia

The Republic of Serbia is a country in southeastern Europe. Its national
authorities oversee personal data protection and financial services.

## What they offer

- **Data protection:** the Law on Personal Data Protection, supervised by the
  Commissioner for Information of Public Importance and Personal Data
  Protection.
- **Financial regulation:** the National Bank of Serbia (NBS) supervises
  banks, insurers and payment institutions.
