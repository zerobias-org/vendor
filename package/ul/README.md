# UL

UL, originally Underwriters Laboratories, is a safety science organization
founded in the United States in 1894. Its work is carried out by UL
Solutions, which provides testing, inspection and certification, and by UL
Standards & Engagement, which develops UL standards.

## What they offer

- **Testing and certification:** product safety testing and certification,
  including the UL Mark.
- **Standards:** UL safety standards developed by UL Standards & Engagement.
- **Cybersecurity and software:** security testing and assessment of
  connected products, plus software for compliance and sustainability.
- **Inspection and advisory:** inspection, auditing and advisory services.
