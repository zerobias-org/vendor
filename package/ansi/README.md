# ANSI

The American National Standards Institute (ANSI) is a private nonprofit
organization that coordinates the US voluntary consensus standards and
conformity assessment system. It is the US member body of ISO.

## What they offer

- **Standards coordination:** accreditation of US standards developers and
  approval of American National Standards.
- **Conformity assessment accreditation:** accreditation of certification
  bodies, including personnel certification programs under ISO/IEC 17024.
- **International representation:** US representation in ISO and, through the
  US National Committee, in the IEC.
