# South Africa

The Republic of South Africa is a country in southern Africa. In this
catalog it represents South African government bodies and laws that apply
to organizations handling personal information or providing regulated
services in South Africa.

## What they offer

- **Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA):** South Africa's data
  protection law.
- **Information Regulator:** enforces POPIA and the Promotion of Access to
  Information Act (PAIA).
- **Financial Sector Conduct Authority (FSCA):** regulator of market conduct
  in the financial sector.
