# Oregon

The State of Oregon is a US state. In this catalog it represents Oregon laws
and agencies that regulate consumer privacy and data security.

## What they offer

- **Oregon Consumer Privacy Act:** consumer data privacy law enforced by the
  Oregon Department of Justice.
- **Oregon Consumer Information Protection Act:** data security and breach
  notification requirements.
