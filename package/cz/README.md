# Czech Republic

The Czech Republic is a Central European country and European Union member
state. This vendor entry represents the Czech government and its national
regulators.

## What they offer

- **Cybersecurity:** the National Cyber and Information Security Agency
  (NÚKIB) is the national authority for cybersecurity.
- **Data protection:** the Office for Personal Data Protection (ÚOOÚ)
  supervises GDPR compliance.

## Official resources

- Website: https://vlada.gov.cz/
- NÚKIB: https://nukib.gov.cz/
- ÚOOÚ: https://uoou.gov.cz/
