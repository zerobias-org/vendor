# Kinaxis

Kinaxis Inc. is a supply chain management software company based in
Ottawa, Canada. Its platform supports supply chain planning and
decision-making.

## What they offer

- **Supply chain planning:** the Maestro platform for demand, supply
  and inventory planning.
- **Scenario planning:** concurrent planning and what-if analysis
  across the supply chain.
- **Supply chain execution:** tools that connect planning with
  execution.
