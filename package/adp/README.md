# ADP

Automatic Data Processing, Inc. (ADP) is a human capital management company
headquartered in Roseland, New Jersey. It provides payroll, HR and workforce
management services to employers.

## What they offer

- **Payroll and tax:** payroll processing and payroll tax filing services.
- **HR and benefits:** human resources, benefits administration and HR
  outsourcing.
- **Workforce management:** time and attendance, talent and workforce
  analytics.
