# Okta

Okta, Inc. is an identity and access management company headquartered in
San Francisco, California. Its cloud services manage how employees,
partners and customers sign in to applications.

## What they offer

- **Workforce identity:** single sign-on, multi-factor authentication,
  user lifecycle management and access governance for employees and partners.
- **Customer identity (Auth0):** sign-up, login and authorization for
  customer-facing applications.
