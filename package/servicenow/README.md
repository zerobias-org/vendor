# ServiceNow

ServiceNow, Inc. is a software company headquartered in Santa Clara,
California. Its cloud platform automates IT, employee and customer workflows.

## What they offer

- **IT service and operations management:** ITSM, ITOM and IT asset
  management.
- **Governance, risk and compliance:** integrated risk management and
  security operations.
- **Workflow platform:** low-code development on the Now Platform.
