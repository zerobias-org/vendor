# China

The People's Republic of China is a country in East Asia. This vendor
represents the Chinese central government and its regulators as sources of
laws and regulations.

## What they offer

- **Central government portal:** official information of the State Council
  (English edition).
- **Cyberspace Administration of China (CAC):** regulator for cybersecurity,
  data security and personal information protection (PIPL).

## Official resources

- Website: https://english.www.gov.cn/
- CAC: https://www.cac.gov.cn/
