# Indonesia

Indonesia is a Southeast Asian country. This vendor entry represents the
Indonesian government and its national regulators.

## What they offer

- **Data protection:** the Personal Data Protection Law (Law No. 27 of 2022).
- **Cybersecurity:** the National Cyber and Crypto Agency (BSSN).
- **Financial supervision:** the Financial Services Authority (OJK).
