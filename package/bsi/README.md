# BSI

BSI (British Standards Institution) is the national standards body of the
United Kingdom, headquartered in London. It develops standards and provides
certification, assurance and training services.

## What they offer

- **Standards:** development and publication of British Standards and UK
  participation in ISO and IEC.
- **Certification:** management system certification, for example to ISO/IEC
  27001 and ISO 9001.
- **Assurance and training:** auditing, testing and training services.
