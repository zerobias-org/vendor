# United Arab Emirates

The United Arab Emirates (UAE) is a federation of seven emirates on the
Arabian Peninsula. This vendor represents the UAE federal government and its
regulators as sources of laws and regulations.

## What they offer

- **Federal government portal:** official information and services of the UAE
  government (u.ae).
- **Telecommunications and Digital Government Regulatory Authority (TDRA):**
  regulator for the telecommunications sector and digital government.

## Official resources

- Website: https://u.ae/en
- TDRA: https://tdra.gov.ae/
