# CMS

The Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) is a US federal agency
within the Department of Health and Human Services (HHS). It administers
Medicare, Medicaid, CHIP and the Health Insurance Marketplace.

## What they offer

- **Medicare and Medicaid:** administration of federal health coverage
  programs.
- **Conditions of Participation:** health and safety standards providers must
  meet to participate in Medicare and Medicaid.
- **HIPAA administrative simplification:** enforcement of HIPAA transaction
  and code set standards.
