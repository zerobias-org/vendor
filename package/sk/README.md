# Slovak Republic

The Slovak Republic is a European Union member state. EU regulations such as
the GDPR apply in Slovakia alongside national law, supervised by Slovak
authorities.

## What they offer

- **Data protection:** the Office for Personal Data Protection supervises
  GDPR compliance.
- **Cybersecurity:** the National Security Authority (NBÚ) is the national
  cybersecurity authority, with SK-CERT as the national CSIRT.

## Official resources

- Central government portal: https://www.slovensko.sk/
- Office for Personal Data Protection: https://dataprotection.gov.sk/
- National Security Authority: https://www.nbu.gov.sk/
