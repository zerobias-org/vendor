# Naverisk

Naverisk is an IT services automation software company. Its platform
combines remote monitoring and management (RMM), service desk and
professional services automation (PSA) for managed service providers
and IT teams.

## What they offer

- **Remote monitoring and management:** monitoring, automation and
  remote access for endpoints.
- **Service desk:** ticketing and service management.
- **PSA:** billing and business management for MSPs.
