# Clockify

Clockify is a time tracking and timesheet application made by CAKE.com.

## What they offer

- **Time tracking:** timers and timesheets for individuals and teams.
- **Reporting:** reports on time, projects and billable hours.
- **Team management:** scheduling, time off, approvals and expenses.
