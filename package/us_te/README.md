# Tennessee

The State of Tennessee is a US state. In this catalog it represents
Tennessee laws and agencies that regulate consumer privacy and data
security.

## What they offer

- **Tennessee Information Protection Act:** consumer data privacy law
  enforced by the Tennessee Attorney General.
- **Data breach notification:** state requirements for notifying residents
  of breaches of personal information.
