# SAICO

SAICO (SCR Assessor and Instructor Certification Organization) certifies
cybersecurity professionals who implement, design and assess programs based
on the Secure Controls Framework (SCF). It is associated with the SCF Council.

## What they offer

- **SCR Practitioner:** certification for professionals who implement
  programs.
- **SCR Architect:** certification for professionals who design programs.
- **SCR Assessor:** certification for conformity assessors.
- **Training:** courses and exams through its training portal.
