# Infor

Infor is an enterprise software company headquartered in New York City and a
subsidiary of Koch Industries. It provides industry-specific cloud business
applications.

## What they offer

- **Cloud ERP:** Infor CloudSuite applications for manufacturing,
  distribution, healthcare, public sector and other industries.
- **Supply chain and HCM:** warehouse management, supply chain and human
  capital management software.
- **Infor OS:** the technology platform for integration, data and automation.
