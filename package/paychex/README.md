# Paychex

Paychex, Inc. is a human capital management company headquartered in
Rochester, New York. It provides payroll, HR, benefits and insurance
services, and acquired Paycor in 2025.

## What they offer

- **Payroll:** payroll processing and tax administration.
- **HR services:** HR software and professional employer organization
  (PEO) services.
- **Benefits:** retirement plans and employee benefits.
- **Insurance:** business insurance services.
