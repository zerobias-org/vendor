# Mango Technologies, Inc. (ClickUp)

Mango Technologies, Inc. is the company behind ClickUp, a productivity
and project management platform. ClickUp combines tasks, documents,
chat and AI in one workspace.

## What they offer

- **Project and task management:** tasks, lists, boards and timelines.
- **Docs and knowledge:** documents and wikis connected to work items.
- **Collaboration:** chat, whiteboards and dashboards.
- **AI features:** AI assistance across tasks and documents.
