# Illinois

The State of Illinois is a US state. In this catalog it represents Illinois
laws and agencies that regulate privacy and data security.

## What they offer

- **Biometric Information Privacy Act (BIPA):** rules on the collection,
  use and retention of biometric identifiers.
- **Personal Information Protection Act:** data security and breach
  notification requirements.
- **Attorney General:** enforces consumer protection and privacy laws.
