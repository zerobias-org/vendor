# Zoho

Zoho Corporation is a software company headquartered in Chennai, India. It
offers a large suite of cloud business applications and, through its
ManageEngine division, IT management software.

## What they offer

- **Zoho One:** an integrated suite of business applications.
- **CRM and sales:** Zoho CRM and related sales and marketing tools.
- **Finance and HR:** accounting (Zoho Books), payroll and HR applications.
- **Collaboration:** email, documents and team communication tools.
- **ManageEngine:** IT operations, security and service management
  software.
