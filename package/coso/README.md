# COSO

The Committee of Sponsoring Organizations of the Treadway Commission (COSO) is
a joint initiative of five US private-sector organizations. It develops
frameworks and guidance on internal control, enterprise risk management and
fraud deterrence.

## What they offer

- **Internal Control - Integrated Framework:** the widely used framework for
  internal control, including for SOX compliance.
- **Enterprise Risk Management framework:** guidance on integrating risk with
  strategy and performance.
- **Fraud deterrence guidance:** guidance on fraud risk management.
