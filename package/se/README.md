# Sweden

The Kingdom of Sweden is a European Union member state. EU regulations such as
the GDPR apply in Sweden alongside national law, supervised by Swedish
authorities.

## What they offer

- **Data protection:** the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY)
  supervises GDPR compliance.
- **Financial supervision:** Finansinspektionen (FI) supervises banks,
  insurers and securities markets.
