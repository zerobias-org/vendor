# Costa Rica

Costa Rica is a country in Central America. This vendor represents the
Government of Costa Rica and its regulators as sources of laws and
regulations.

## What they offer

- **Presidency of the Republic:** official site of the Costa Rican Presidency.
- **PRODHAB:** the Agency for the Protection of Personal Data of Inhabitants,
  under Law 8968.

## Official resources

- Website: https://www.presidencia.go.cr/
- PRODHAB: https://www.prodhab.go.cr/
