# HCI

HCI makes patient engagement and clinical communication technology for
healthcare facilities.

## What they offer

- **HCI ONE:** a patient room platform that runs the patient TV, digital
  whiteboard, door sign and unit board.
- **Integrations:** fed by the electronic medical record (EMR) and nurse call
  systems.
- **Deployment:** on-premises, installed through dealers.
