# AICPA

The American Institute of Certified Public Accountants (AICPA) is the US
professional association for certified public accountants. Together with CIMA
it forms the Association of International Certified Professional Accountants.

## What they offer

- **Auditing and attestation standards:** standards and guidance used by CPAs
  in the United States.
- **SOC reporting:** the System and Organization Controls (SOC) framework,
  including SOC 1, SOC 2 and SOC 3 reports.
- **Trust Services Criteria:** criteria for security, availability, processing
  integrity, confidentiality and privacy used in SOC 2.
