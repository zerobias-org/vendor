# Rippling

Rippling is a workforce management platform that unifies HR, IT and finance
operations for employees in one system.

## What they offer

- **HR and payroll:** payroll, benefits, time tracking and HR management.
- **IT management:** device management, app provisioning and identity.
- **Spend management:** corporate cards, expenses and bill pay.
