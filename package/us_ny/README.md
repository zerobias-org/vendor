# New York

The State of New York is a US state. In this catalog it represents New York
laws and agencies that regulate cybersecurity, privacy and financial
services.

## What they offer

- **NYDFS Cybersecurity Regulation (23 NYCRR 500):** cybersecurity
  requirements for financial services companies regulated by the Department
  of Financial Services.
- **SHIELD Act:** data security and breach notification requirements for
  businesses holding private information of New York residents.
- **Attorney General:** enforces consumer protection and data security laws.
