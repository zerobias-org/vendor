# Salesforce

Salesforce, Inc. is a cloud software company headquartered in San Francisco,
California, best known for its customer relationship management (CRM)
platform. Its portfolio includes Slack, Tableau and MuleSoft.

## What they offer

- **CRM applications:** Sales Cloud, Service Cloud, Marketing Cloud and
  Commerce Cloud.
- **Platform:** low-code and developer tools for building on Salesforce.
- **AI and data:** AI agents and a customer data platform.
