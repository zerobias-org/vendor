# Asana

Asana, Inc. is a work management software company headquartered in San
Francisco, California.

## What they offer

- **Work management:** projects, tasks, portfolios and goals for teams.
- **Workflow automation:** rules, forms and integrations to automate work.
- **AI features:** AI assistance for planning and managing work.
