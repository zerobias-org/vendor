# Ireland

Ireland is a European Union member state. This vendor entry represents the
Irish government and its national regulators.

## What they offer

- **Data protection:** the Data Protection Commission (DPC), the lead GDPR
  supervisory authority for many multinational technology companies.
- **Cybersecurity:** the National Cyber Security Centre (NCSC) handles
  national incident response and cybersecurity guidance.
- **Financial regulation:** the Central Bank of Ireland regulates financial
  services firms.
