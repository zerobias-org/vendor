# Taiwan

Taiwan is a jurisdiction in East Asia. In this catalog it represents
Taiwanese government bodies and laws that apply to organizations handling
personal data or operating in regulated sectors in Taiwan.

## What they offer

- **Personal Data Protection Act:** Taiwan's main law on the collection,
  processing and use of personal data.
- **Ministry of Digital Affairs:** responsible for digital policy and, through
  its Administration for Cyber Security, the Cyber Security Management Act.
- **Financial Supervisory Commission:** regulator of banking, securities and
  insurance.
