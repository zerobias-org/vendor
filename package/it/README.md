# Italy

Italy is a European Union member state. This vendor entry represents the
Italian government and its national regulators.

## What they offer

- **Data protection:** the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali
  supervises GDPR compliance.
- **Cybersecurity:** the National Cybersecurity Agency (ACN) oversees national
  cybersecurity, including NIS2 and cloud qualification for public
  administration.
- **Digital government:** the Agency for Digital Italy (AgID) sets technical
  rules for public sector IT.
