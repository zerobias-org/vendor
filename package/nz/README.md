# New Zealand

This vendor entry represents the Government of New Zealand as a source
of national laws, regulations and regulators relevant to compliance.

## What they offer

- **Office of the Privacy Commissioner:** the regulator for the Privacy
  Act 2020.
- **National Cyber Security Centre (NCSC):** the government's lead
  cybersecurity agency.

## Official resources

- Website: https://www.govt.nz/
- Office of the Privacy Commissioner: https://www.privacy.org.nz/
- NCSC: https://www.ncsc.govt.nz/
