# California

The State of California is a US state. In this catalog it represents
California laws and agencies that regulate privacy, data security and
related compliance obligations.

## What they offer

- **California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):** consumer privacy law, as
  amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA).
- **California Privacy Protection Agency:** implements and enforces the
  CCPA together with the Attorney General.
- **Data breach notification:** California's breach notification
  requirements, with breach reports published by the Attorney General.
