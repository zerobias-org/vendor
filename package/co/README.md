# Colombia

Colombia is a country in South America. This vendor represents the Government
of Colombia and its regulators as sources of laws and regulations.

## What they offer

- **Government portal:** official information and services of the Colombian
  government (gov.co).
- **Superintendencia de Industria y Comercio (SIC):** the data protection
  authority under Law 1581 of 2012.

## Official resources

- Website: https://www.gov.co/
- SIC: https://www.sic.gov.co/
