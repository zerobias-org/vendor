# Blue Yonder

Blue Yonder is a supply chain management software company headquartered in
Scottsdale, Arizona. It is a subsidiary of Panasonic.

## What they offer

- **Supply chain planning:** demand and supply planning.
- **Warehouse and logistics:** warehouse management and transportation
  management.
- **Commerce and retail:** order management and merchandising.
