# Venmo

Venmo is a mobile payment service in the United States owned by PayPal. It
lets people send and receive money and pay businesses from a mobile app.

## What they offer

- **Peer-to-peer payments:** sending and receiving money between friends,
  with a social feed.
- **Business payments:** business profiles and checkout for merchants.
- **Venmo cards:** debit and credit cards linked to a Venmo account.
